June 21, 2026

Creators were paid $2,000 to $3,000 per month and told not to disclose the relationship. A marketing firm called Virality managed a clipper network: mostly teenagers in Asia, reposting content on fresh accounts for $1 per 1,000 views, paid only when 60 percent of their audience was American. Polymarket has been barred from serving US users since a 2022 CFTC settlement.

The Adin Ross Deal

Adin Ross, 25 million followers, has a multimillion-dollar deal with Polymarket. In at least five videos he identified on camera how inside information could be used to win on the platform. He called it information arbitrage. Polymarket and Virality promoted those clips.

The Human Story

50 People Lost the Bet George Makihara Faked Winning

More than 50 real accounts placed the Trump McDonald's bet in January 2026. Makihara's videos showed him winning $100,000 on that position on a counterfeit website. All 50 lost.

These are people who watched a college student appear to win $100K on a simple bet, placed the same wager on the real platform, and lost real money on a position manufactured to look like free money. Their blockchain data is public. They are the center of this film.

June 25, 2026

The $3.1 Million Hack

A third-party vendor supplying frontend code to Polymarket was compromised. Attackers injected a malicious script that tricked users into signing unauthorized transactions. On-chain tracking confirmed approximately $3.1 million drained from 11 to 15 wallets, converted to 1,893 ETH and moved through attacker-controlled addresses. Polymarket pledged full refunds to all affected users.

The Named Victim

A user named Ash posted on X in real time as his wallet was drained. He shared both wallet addresses publicly, giving on-chain investigators the data to begin tracking. He is the only victim named in public reporting. Ten others lost funds without being identified.

June 26, 2026

Three Escalations. One Day.

CFTC
Expanded Federal Probe
Bloomberg reports the CFTC is conducting a broad probe into Polymarket's operations and social media activity. The prior investigation was closed in July 2025 with no charges. CFTC Chair Michael Selig's ethics recusal from former crypto clients expires mid-2026.
Federal Lawsuit
CEO and CMO Named Personally
Vaca Daffan Law files on behalf of the National Association of Consumer Advocates. Named defendants: Blockratize (parent), CEO Shayne Coplan, and CMO Matthew Modabber personally. Allegation: a sweeping and flagrantly deceptive campaign targeting college-aged individuals.
Senate
Curtis and Schiff Joint Letter
Senators John Curtis (R-UT) and Adam Schiff (D-CA) write jointly to CFTC Chairman Selig calling the allegations deeply troubling. They demand a written response by July 10, 2026. The same pair introduced legislation targeting sports wagers on CFTC-regulated platforms.

Through all three escalations, Polymarket reported revenue crossing one billion dollars.

Why It Matters

The CFTC now probing Polymarket is the same CFTC that cleared it in July 2025. The chair previously represented Polymarket-adjacent clients at Willkie Farr. Donald Trump Jr. holds equity in Polymarket and sits on its advisory board. He is simultaneously a paid strategic adviser to Kalshi, Polymarket's primary regulated rival. The regulatory pressure landing on June 26 is hitting a platform at the center of the Trump financial network.
